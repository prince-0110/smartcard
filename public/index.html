FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need to reprint if my details change?

No. The code on your card points to your account, so whatever your profile shows today is what people see.

Do visitors need an app?

No. Phone cameras open the link directly, and NFC phones open it with a tap.

What do you track?

Only that a scan happened, when, and whether it came from QR, NFC or a direct link, plus mobile or desktop. No IP addresses.

Can I upload my own design?

Yes. Send PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF for each side. We show a card preview and flag size or resolution problems before you order.
